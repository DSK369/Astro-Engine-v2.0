## 1. Executive Summary
**Project Name:** Geocentric Ephemeris Visualizer (400-Year Epoch)
**Objective:** To provide an interactive, web-based graphical representation of planetary motion as seen from Earth, covering a timeline from **-200 to +200 years**. The system must visualize the cyclical nature of the planets through the 12 Rashis (Zodiac signs), including mathematically accurate retrograde loops.

## 2. Mathematical Model & Logic
The engine must simulate the apparent motion of celestial bodies from a geocentric (Earth-centered) perspective using the principle of **Epicyclic Oscillation**.

### 2.1 The Coordinate Engine
For each planet, the longitude $\theta(t)$ must be calculated using a superposition of mean motion and synodic perturbation:
$$\theta(t) = (n \cdot t + \phi + A \cdot \sin(\frac{2\pi \cdot t}{T_{syn}})) \pmod{360}$$

* **$n$ (Mean Motion):** Average forward progress through the zodiac.
* **$A$ (Amplitude):** The "depth" of the retrograde loop.
* **$T_{syn}$ (Synodic Period):** The interval between successive retrograde occurrences.
* **$\phi$ (Phase Offset):** Alignment for the starting epoch (Year 0).

### 2.2 Planetary Constants (Conceptual)
| Body | Mean Motion (n) | Synodic Period ($T_{syn}$) | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sun** | $360^\circ / 1y$ | N/A | Constant forward wave. |
| **Moon** | $360^\circ / 27.3d$ | N/A | High-frequency cycle. |
| **Mercury** | $360^\circ / 1y$ | ~116 days | Fast, tight retrograde loops. |
| **Jupiter** | $360^\circ / 11.86y$ | ~399 days | Slow motion, large annual loops. |

## 3. Technical Stack & Architecture

### 3.1 Hybrid Rendering Strategy
To maintain **60 FPS** while panning across 146,000+ data points (400 years of daily data), a hybrid approach is mandatory:
* **HTML5 Canvas:** All planetary "Trace Lines." SVG cannot handle the DOM node count required for 400 years of Moon data.
* **SVG Layer:** Axes, Rashi labels, and the interactive "Scrubbing" playhead.
* **D3.js:** Managing the coordinate scales (`scaleLinear`) and the complex Zoom/Pan behaviors.

### 3.2 State & Performance
* **Variable Sampling:** Implement a dynamic step rate. Use $0.1$ day steps for the Moon/Mercury and $5$ day steps for Saturn/Jupiter to optimize memory.
* **Path Segmentation Logic:** The renderer must detect "Angular Discontinuity." If $\Delta y > 180^\circ$, the canvas must `moveTo()` instead of `lineTo()` to prevent the line from crossing the screen during a $360^\circ \to 0^\circ$ transition.

## 4. UI/UX Functional Requirements

### 4.1 The Y-Axis (Rashi Mapping)
The Y-axis is fixed from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$, divided into 12 bands of $30^\circ$ each.
* **Labels:** Mesha (Aries), Vrishabha (Taurus), Mithuna (Gemini), Karka (Cancer), Simha (Leo), Kanya (Virgo), Tula (Libra), Vrishchika (Scorpio), Dhanu (Sagittarius), Makara (Capricorn), Kumbha (Aquarius), and Meena (Pisces).
* **Visual Aid:** Alternating background fills for each Rashi band.

### 4.2 The X-Axis (Timeline)
* **Range:** Absolute years from -200 to +200.
* **Interactions:** * **Smooth Zooming:** Transition from a "Centennial Overview" to a "Daily Transit" view.
    * **Tooltips:** On hover, show precise Rashi name, degree, and date.

### 4.3 Controls & Customization
* **Planet Toggles:** Multi-select legend to isolate specific planet interactions.
* **Ayanamsa Toggle:** Ability to shift between Tropical (Sayana) and Sidereal (Nirayana) systems (a constant $24^\circ$ offset for the modern era).
